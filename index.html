The problem

Something in your business runs on patches and workarounds

Maybe your spreadsheet started as something simple and grew into a tangle no one can make sense of. Maybe your Google Drive is a mess of duplicate folders and half-finished files. Maybe there's a process only you understand, and it slows everything down.

  • A donor or grant tracker three people update differently, so nobody fully trusts the numbers
  • A volunteer sign-up process that only really lives in someone's inbox
  • A shared Drive with folders named "Final," "Final v2," and "Use This One"
  • A client onboarding process that changes slightly every time because it's never been written down
  • A booking calendar that doesn't talk to your invoicing, so you're doing the math twice
  • A stack of program enrollment paperwork with no way to tell what's missing until it's too late

Sound familiar? You know it needs to be fixed. You don't have the time, or the technical background, to fix it yourself.

What I do about it

One call. One fix. Handed to you, ready to use

  • We start with a live diagnostic call, not a long back-and-forth over email
  • You get a written plan and a fixed price before any work begins
  • I build the fix myself and hand it over ready to use, no ongoing back-and-forth required
  • If the real problem is a process only you understand, I can write it down as a clear procedure so it's not just in your head anymore
