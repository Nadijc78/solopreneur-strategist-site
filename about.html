About

I use Google Workspace to turn messy business problems into simple, working systems

I've spent over sixteen years in information management, working everywhere from large professional services firms to small businesses and non-profits. I kept seeing the same thing: smart people overwhelmed with too many spreadsheets or inadequate systems that used to work but had slowly become a source of daily friction. I started The Solopreneur Strategist to help fix exactly that, one small project at a time.

Why I started this: I watched too many capable business owners lose time and energy to clunky systems and disjointed processes because they didn't have the time or the technical background to do it themselves.

Credentials: My background includes a Master of Science in Library and Information Science, along with certifications as a Certified Knowledge Manager and Certified Information Professional. Before any of that, I spent time as a Peace Corps volunteer in Cameroon, which is part of where my pull toward service-driven work, especially with non-profits, first started.

How I build

Simple tools over shiny ones

Google Workspace is where most of what I build lives, along with the tools that connect to it. I also use AI where it genuinely speeds things up, not to replace critical thinking, but to support it. I'm a systems person first, so the platform serves the problem, not the other way around. I don't default to the priciest or trendiest option just because it's popular. A lot of what's marketed to small businesses is built for a scale you don't have yet: monthly subscriptions stacked on subscriptions, five different logins for five different jobs.

Sometimes the fix isn't a new tool at all. It's a fresh perspective on the workflow you already have, or a solid, repeatable process that's never been written down. Other times it really is a well-built spreadsheet, Google Form, or custom app. My focus is finding the simplest, yet efficient, thing that actually solves your problem now, with room to grow into something more when you need it.

I won't pretend I've tried every tool on the market. Since starting this business in 2019, I've fallen for my share of shiny new tools, and most of them never made it past the free trial. That's the lens I bring to every recommendation.
